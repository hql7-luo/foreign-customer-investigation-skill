# Printing Product Matching Guide

## Mandatory logic

Use:

```text
customer's verified product or equipment
→ printing process
→ consumable or maintenance need
→ one to three specific product categories
```

For every recommendation, include:

- Customer product or equipment
- Printing process
- Specific need
- Recommended product
- Reason
- Parameters to confirm
- Evidence status

Do not list the entire catalog. If process/equipment is not reliable, recommend only a verification step or a broad category clearly marked as inference.

## Product scope

- Printing consumables
- Offset ink
- UV ink
- LED UV products
- Printing plates
- Blankets
- Fountain solution
- Cleaning agents
- Printing-equipment spare parts
- Cooling systems and related parts
- Other printing, packaging, and equipment-maintenance products

## Process mappings

| Customer evidence | Process/application | Likely need | Candidate recommendation | Parameters to confirm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sheetfed commercial/book printing | Offset lithography | Color, transfer, pressroom consumables | Offset ink; printing plates; blankets | Press brand/model, substrate, color standard, plate size, blanket size |
| Folding cartons with UV curing | UV offset | Adhesion, cure, rub resistance | UV ink; UV-compatible blankets; cleaning agent | Lamp type, substrate, ink series, cure speed, resistance requirement |
| LED UV press or retrofit | LED UV offset/digital hybrid | Wavelength-compatible curing and heat management | LED UV product; compatible ink; cooling-system parts | Wavelength, irradiance, press width, cooling load, electrical interface |
| Label production | Flexo, offset, digital, screen, or UV | Ink/plate/cleaning depends on actual process | Recommend only after process confirmation; up to three verified categories | Press/process, substrate, ink chemistry, curing, color requirement |
| Flexible packaging | Flexo or gravure | Ink and cleaning compatibility; compliance | Recommend only products within seller scope and supported process | Film type, printing method, lamination, food-contact requirement, solvent/water/UV system |
| Metal decoration | Offset/UV/specialty coating | Adhesion and cure on metal | UV ink or process-compatible consumable when supported | Metal type/coating, oven/UV system, resistance tests |
| Equipment distributor/service company | Multiple | Spare parts and maintenance consumables | Equipment spare parts; cleaning agents; cooling components | Installed base, exact model, part number, annual service demand |
| Packaging/printing end user outsourcing production | No owned print process confirmed | Vendor-management need rather than direct consumable use | Do not recommend press consumables until ownership/use is confirmed | In-house vs outsourced, supplier role, approval authority |

## Decision rules

1. Treat exact equipment models as facts only when a reliable source or customer documentation supports them.
2. When no model is found, write: “未找到公开设备型号，需向客户直接确认 / No public equipment model was found; confirm directly with the customer.”
3. Distinguish production use from resale/distribution use.
4. Match chemistry and compliance requirements to substrate and application.
5. Avoid recommending UV or LED UV products merely because “UV” appears in unrelated marketing text.
6. For regulated applications such as food packaging, state that technical and regulatory documentation must be reviewed; do not imply compliance without product-specific evidence.
7. Prefer the smallest viable entry product with clear test parameters.

## Examples

### Supported

`[Confirmed] The company publicly shows a sheetfed offset carton line.`

Recommendation:

- Customer application: folding carton
- Process: sheetfed offset
- Need: repeat ink and blanket consumption
- Product: offset ink and blanket
- Confirm: press model, colors, substrate, blanket dimensions, monthly test quantity

### Inference

`[Inference] A packaging converter advertising UV-varnished cartons may use UV offset or offline coating; the exact process is not confirmed.`

Recommendation:

- Do not quote a full UV-ink range.
- First request equipment, curing, substrate, and current product specifications.
